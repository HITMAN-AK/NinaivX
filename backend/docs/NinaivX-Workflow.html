NinaivX

A voice app to talk with the people who matter — even those we've lost.
You speak, and an AI answers back in a real human voice — like a phone call.
In one sentence: NinaivX lets a person hold a natural, spoken conversation with an AI — either a caring companion for someone who feels lonely, or a comforting recreation of a loved one who has passed away, speaking in a voice you choose or even their own cloned voice.
Who it is for — two kinds of conversation

🕊️ The Loved One (Legacy)

For someone grieving. They can talk to an AI version of a person who has passed away — to feel close again, say the things left unsaid, and find comfort and closure.

🔒 Kept offline so it can never invent false things about the person

💬 The Companion

For someone lonely. A warm, supportive friend available any time, that listens and talks naturally — and can even chat about today's news or weather.

🌐 Connected to the internet for up-to-date conversation
Getting started (done once)
1
Create an account and sign in.  Simple email and password — this keeps every person's data private and separate.
2
Describe the person.  Their name, personality, and how they spoke — this is what makes the AI feel like them.
3
Choose a voice.  Say the kind of voice you want (for example a man or woman, younger or older) and pick from the options — or upload a short clip to clone their real voice.

➜

➜
💾

5. It remembers

The chat is saved for next time.

The clever part — the “brain” makes a safety decision. Before replying, the app checks who you are talking to. If it is a loved one, it stays completely offline, so it can never make up false “facts” about a real person who has died. If it is a companion, it is allowed to look things up online. This safety choice is the heart of the project.
Our promises to the user
🔒 PrivateYour conversations and the person's details are protected, and each user only ever sees their own.
🙂 HonestThe app always makes clear it is an AI — it will never pretend to truly be the real person.
🕊️ GentleBuilt to support healthy grieving and comfort — not to replace the person or trap you.
